# Holistic Learning Model

## What is holistic learning?

Holistic learning is an approach that focuses on a learner's overall development, not only academic knowledge. It can include practical skills, critical thinking, communication, collaboration, emotional development, and problem-solving.

## Example

A student does not learn only by reading a textbook. They may also practise a task, work with a team, communicate their ideas, solve a problem, and reflect on what they could improve.

## How it connects to learning cloud computing

While studying cloud computing, holistic learning can include:
- **Knowledge:** Understanding cloud concepts and terminology.
- **Practical skills:** Creating and configuring cloud resources in a lab.
- **Problem-solving:** Investigating errors and learning how to troubleshoot them.
- **Communication:** Explaining technical concepts clearly.
- **Teamwork:** Collaborating on exercises and sharing useful findings.
- **Reflection:** Recording what worked, what did not, and what to learn next.

## My learning approach

I aim to connect theory with practice, explain concepts in simple language, document my progress, and improve through feedback and troubleshooting.

## Key takeaway

Learning is more effective when knowledge is combined with practice, communication, collaboration, and reflection.
